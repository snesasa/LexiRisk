EXCLUSIVE COMMERCIAL DISTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Commercial Distributor Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of March 1, 2026, by and between Zenith Manufacturing Corp. ("Manufacturer") and Apex Distribution Network LLC ("Distributor").

1. APPOINTMENT AND EXCLUSIVITY
1.1 Manufacturer hereby appoints Distributor as its sole and exclusive distributor of the Products within the United Kingdom and Germany (the "Territory"). Manufacturer covenants that it shall not sell the Products directly or through any other distributor within the Territory during the term.

2. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENTS
2.1 Distributor agrees to purchase a minimum annual volume of 50,000 units of Products ("Minimum Commitment"). If Distributor fails to achieve the Minimum Commitment in any calendar year, Manufacturer may, at its option, convert this Agreement to a non-exclusive distribution contract or terminate immediately upon written notice.

3. MOST FAVORED NATION (MFN) PRICING
3.1 Manufacturer guarantees that unit prices charged to Distributor shall be no less favorable than the lowest prices offered to any other distributor globally for equivalent purchase volumes ("Most Favored Nation").

4. AUDIT RIGHTS AND INSPECTION
4.1 Manufacturer shall have the right, upon fifteen (15) days written notice, to examine and audit Distributor's sales records, warehouses, and books during normal business hours to verify compliance with territorial restrictions and pricing covenants. If any audit reveals underreporting of over 3%, Distributor shall reimburse Manufacturer for the full cost of the audit.

5. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
5.1 Any unauthorized sale of Products outside the Territory by Distributor shall constitute a material breach, and Distributor shall pay Manufacturer liquidated damages of $500 per unit sold outside the Territory, which the parties agree represents a genuine pre-estimate of loss.

6. TERMINATION AND POST-TERMINATION OBLIGATIONS
6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of receipt of written notice.
6.2 Upon termination, Distributor shall immediately cease all marketing and shall sell off remaining inventory within sixty (60) days.
